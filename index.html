About WELL Lab

Water–Energy–Environment Nexus

The Water & Electrochemical Engineering Laboratory (WELL Lab) is dedicated to advancing sustainable water and environmental technologies through interdisciplinary research.

Guided by the Water–Energy–Environment Nexus, our research integrates innovative materials, electrochemical technologies, and numerical modeling to develop efficient and environmentally sustainable solutions.

We aim to promote sustainable water management, enhance energy efficiency, and advance resource recovery and circular economy practices. Our work also addresses water quality and emerging environmental challenges, contributing to the long-term sustainability of aquatic ecosystems and the environment.

Research Framework

Research

Electrokinetic Processes

Investigation of electrokinetic phenomena and advanced electrochemical processes for water treatment, resource recovery, and sustainable water technologies. Development and application of charged porous materials for ion transport, selective separation, and electrochemical processes.

Numerical Simulation

Development of multiphysics numerical models to investigate ion transport, fluid dynamics, and electrokinetic phenomena in electrochemical separation systems. By coupling the Nernst–Planck equations, fluid flow, and electrochemical processes, we aim to elucidate underlying transport mechanisms, optimize reactor configurations, and improve separation efficiency and energy performance through computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and numerical simulation.
